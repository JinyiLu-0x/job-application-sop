# Resume SOP

## Review order

1. Confirm the current source file and target role.
2. Extract all text and visually inspect the rendered page.
3. Check facts, dates, work rights, education, grades, links, project status, and future programs.
4. Compare the resume with the job advertisement.
5. Recommend only changes that improve fit, accuracy, clarity, or readability.
6. Preserve the existing visual system and one-page constraint unless the user asks for a redesign.

## Surgical tailoring

Make small, targeted adjustments in this order:

1. Summary positioning.
2. Section and project order.
3. Wording of the most relevant bullets.
4. Skills or keywords genuinely supported by evidence.
5. Removal of less relevant detail when space is required.

Do not rewrite the entire resume for every role. Explain each proposed change with:

1. Current wording or location.
2. Problem.
3. Exact replacement.
4. Why it improves this application.

## Positioning by role

### Business Analysis or technology consulting

Lead with process improvement, stakeholder or user needs, structured problem solving, data or workflow thinking, documentation, and the ability to understand technical constraints. Use software development as supporting evidence.

### Software development

Lead with implementation, architecture, APIs, databases, testing, deployment, and measurable project scope. Keep business context when it explains why the software matters.

### AI, automation, or product

Lead with practical use cases, workflow design, prototypes, evaluation, human review, and responsible implementation. Do not imply model research or production experience without evidence.

## Evidence rules

1. Use action plus scope plus result where the result is verifiable.
2. Do not invent metrics.
3. Keep incoming programs in future tense.
4. Describe design work as design work.
5. Keep tool lists selective and relevant.
6. Do not call a prototype a mature product.
7. Ensure portfolio, GitHub, and LinkedIn links are correct and clickable.

## Accuracy gate for work rights

Visa expiry and work conditions are different facts. A temporary visa valid until a future year does not automatically mean full or unlimited work rights. If the resume contains a work-right statement, verify it against the current visa or VEVO evidence. Replace or remove any statement that conflicts with current conditions before submission.

## Visual checks

1. One A4 page when agreed.
2. Consistent margins, headings, dates, spacing, and bullet indentation.
3. No clipped text, broken glyphs, duplicated bullets, or orphaned headings.
4. Readable at normal zoom.
5. ATS-readable text with standard section names.
6. A clean filename containing the candidate name and document type.
