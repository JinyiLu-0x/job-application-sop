# Output Contract

## Role research

Return:

1. What the company and team or program actually do.
2. What the role appears to need.
3. Eligibility and deadline.
4. Candidate fit with evidence.
5. Risks or missing facts.
6. Recommended positioning.

Use concise Chinese. Cite current authoritative sources when research was required.

## Form question

Return only the current question in this format:

**题目意思：**

**建议填写：**

**原因：**

**需要确认：** only when necessary.

Then wait for the user's response before moving on.

## Cover letter

Drafting sequence:

1. Chinese outline and evidence choices.
2. English draft.
3. Local revision with the exact changed passage.
4. Full approved text on request.
5. Final one-page Word document and PDF only when requested.

## Resume review

Group findings by severity:

1. Must fix: factual, eligibility, legal, broken-link, or serious credibility problem.
2. Should fix: role fit, clarity, grammar, or layout problem.
3. Optional: preference or minor polish.

For each finding, include the exact replacement when possible.

## Final review

Return:

1. Ready or not ready.
2. Blocking issues.
3. Changes made.
4. Files prepared.
5. The next action.
