# Communication SOP

Use this guide for recruiter emails, application messages, interview preparation, clarification questions, and live collaboration with the user.

## Working with the user

1. Explain unfamiliar wording in Chinese before recommending an answer.
2. Lead with what the question means in this application, not a dictionary translation.
3. Give one recommended answer and a short reason.
4. When filling a form, wait for the user's answer, then move to the next question.
5. Do not dump all remaining questions unless the user asks for a full answer sheet.
6. If a fact is unknown, say exactly what must be checked. Do not guess.
7. When the user corrects a fact or preference, update the answer and preserve the rest.

## Recruiter or employer message

Use this structure:

1. Subject that names the role or application purpose.
2. Appropriate greeting. Use a person's name only when verified.
3. One sentence stating the application, event, or question.
4. One or two sentences giving the most relevant context.
5. A clear action, such as reviewing an attachment, confirming eligibility, or answering a question.
6. A short courteous close and the candidate's name.

Keep most messages under 150 words. Identify attachments by function, not by a long description. Never add claims that are absent from the resume or user confirmation.

## Tone checks

Before sending, verify that the message is:

1. Direct but polite.
2. Specific to the recipient and purpose.
3. Free of generic enthusiasm and unnecessary personal history.
4. Grammatically correct in Australian English.
5. Consistent with the application documents.
