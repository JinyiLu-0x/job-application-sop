# Application Quality Gates

Complete the applicable gates before calling an application final.

## Source accuracy

1. Exact company, role title, program, stream, and location match the advertisement.
2. No invented recruiter, team, manager, value, technology, or responsibility.
3. Application dates and program dates are current.
4. Employment type and pay format match the source.

## Candidate truth

1. Degree, major, institution, expected graduation date, WAM, GPA, and awards match current evidence.
2. Work-right wording matches current visa conditions, not merely the visa expiry date.
3. Projects, tools, roles, and results are not overstated.
4. Future activities are written in future tense.
5. Every cover-letter example appears in the resume, portfolio, or confirmed user history.

## Cross-document consistency

1. Name, contact information, location, degree, graduation date, and links agree across all files and forms.
2. Role title and company spelling agree across the cover letter, filenames, and form.
3. Salary, availability, location, and employment-status answers do not conflict with the advertisement.
4. Form answers do not contradict the resume or cover letter.

## Writing quality

1. The opening is direct.
2. Claims have concrete support.
3. The employer connection is specific and verified.
4. Sentences are concise and professional.
5. Repetition, especially "also", is controlled.
6. No generic company praise, inflated adjectives, or robotic transitions.
7. Australian spelling and punctuation are consistent.
8. Dash punctuation is avoided in prose.

## Layout and file checks

1. Resume and cover letter meet the agreed page limit.
2. The final Word or PDF is rendered and visually inspected.
3. Margins, spacing, bullets, alignment, and font size are consistent.
4. No blank extra page, clipping, overlap, or broken links.
5. The document contains selectable text and remains ATS-readable.
6. The filename is professional and role-specific when useful.

## Submission pause conditions

Stop and ask the user before submission when:

1. Work rights appear incompatible with a mandatory eligibility requirement.
2. A legal or integrity answer is uncertain.
3. A required document is missing.
4. Two current sources contradict each other.
5. A requested change would materially misrepresent the candidate.
