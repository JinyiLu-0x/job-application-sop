# Opportunity Sourcing Branch

Use this branch when Jinyi asks what to search, wants LinkedIn Posts queries, or supplies a hiring post. Keep it separate from the formal application workflow.

## Two sourcing routes

1. **Regular:** formal online applications from Jobs pages, employer career sites, SEEK, GradConnection, Prosple and similar sources.
2. **Non-standard:** every opportunity first discovered through a LinkedIn post, including posts written by founders, managers, recruiters, employees or company pages. A post may link to a formal application or ask people to contact the author directly.

Do not split LinkedIn Posts into further regular and non-standard categories.

## Posts search method

Follow the user's reference method: combine one quoted hiring phrase with `technology`, one work-arrangement term when useful, and a location marker. Keep queries short.

Core hiring phrases:

- `"Hiring now"`
- `"We're hiring"`
- `"Join our team"`
- `"Career opportunities"`
- `"New opening"`
- `"Team is hiring"`
- `"Open roles"`
- `"Looking for"`

Keep `technology` as the default domain term. Rotate these work-arrangement terms instead of relying on seniority labels:

- `intern`
- `internship`
- `student`
- `vacationer`
- `"part time"` or `part-time`
- `casual`

Prioritise internships, then part-time, casual, student and vacationer opportunities. Do not recommend a full-time-only role unless Jinyi later asks for full-time work.

Do not use `junior`, `senior` or LinkedIn's experience-level label as a hard filter. Those labels are often unreliable. Inspect the actual title, duties, required experience and employment type. An explicitly senior role requiring substantial prior experience is still unsuitable, but an incorrect platform label must not hide an otherwise suitable early-career role.

## Location split

Always divide daily Posts queries into two groups.

### Melbourne onsite or hybrid

Use `Melbourne` with one hiring phrase, `technology` and one work-arrangement term, for example:

- `"We're hiring" technology intern Melbourne`
- `"Join our team" technology internship Melbourne`
- `"Looking for" technology student Melbourne`
- `"Open roles" technology "part time" Melbourne`
- `"New opening" technology casual Melbourne`
- `"Career opportunities" technology vacationer Melbourne`

### Remote anywhere

Use `remote` without restricting the country, for example:

- `"We're hiring" technology intern remote`
- `"Join our team" technology internship remote`
- `"Looking for" technology student remote`
- `"Open roles" technology "part time" remote`
- `"New opening" technology casual remote`
- `"Career opportunities" technology vacationer remote`

Rotate hiring phrases and work-arrangement terms between days. Prefer four to six queries per group. For remote results, later verify that the employer can hire someone based in Australia and that the time zone, payroll and visa conditions are workable.

## Filters and fallback

Select `Posts`, then `Past week`; use `Past 24 hours` for a daily check. Do not require `Content type: Job posts`, because manager or founder posts may not be classified as job posts.

If a query returns nothing, first try removing the quoted hiring phrase while keeping the domain, work arrangement and location:

- `technology intern Melbourne`
- `technology internship remote`

Alternatively, remove only the work-arrangement term and inspect the posts manually:

- `"We're hiring" technology Melbourne`
- `"Join our team" technology remote`

Do not build large Boolean strings by default.

## Output contract

When Jinyi asks for Posts search terms, respond briefly with:

1. `Melbourne onsite / hybrid`: four to six copy-ready queries.
2. `Remote anywhere`: four to six copy-ready queries.
3. One short filter line and one short fallback line.

Do not include formal Jobs queries, long explanations or sources unless requested.

When she asks for a live search, use current web research without opening or controlling her browser unless she explicitly requests browser use. When she supplies a post, verify the author, employer, role, location, work arrangement, application route, fit and eligibility before recommending action. Never message, connect, upload or submit without explicit permission.
