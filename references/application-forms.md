# Application Form SOP

## One-question-at-a-time format

For each question, respond in this order:

1. **题目意思：** explain what the employer is actually asking in one or two Chinese sentences.
2. **建议填写：** give the exact option or text to enter.
3. **原因：** give a short factual reason.
4. **需要确认：** include this only if a missing fact could change the answer.

After the user confirms or answers, continue to the next question.

## Decision rules

### Work rights and visa

Use the exact conditions on the current visa or VEVO record. Distinguish carefully between:

1. Citizen or permanent resident.
2. Unlimited work rights on a temporary visa.
3. Limited work rights on a temporary visa.
4. Requiring employer sponsorship.

Never infer unlimited rights from a visa expiry date. Never advise a false selection to pass screening. If the role requires unrestricted rights and the candidate has limited rights, state the conflict clearly before submission.

### Director

This normally asks whether the applicant is formally a company director, not a film director, club officer, team leader, or design officer. Recommend "No" unless the user confirms a registered directorship or equivalent formal position.

### Optional identity and support questions

Explain the purpose neutrally. For disability, cultural identity, refugee background, and similar questions, give the user control. "Prefer not to respond" is valid when offered. Do not diagnose, pressure, or optimise identity answers for selection.

### Criminal, driving, bankruptcy, and integrity questions

Explain the legal wording, including spent or unspent convictions when relevant, but do not guess. Ask the user to answer truthfully. Recommend professional or legal clarification when the meaning remains uncertain.

### Employment status

Select the candidate's relationship with the named employer, not general employment history. If the candidate has never worked for that employer, choose the employer's exact "Never Employed" option.

### Desired employment status

Follow the advertised role. An internship described as casual should normally use "Casual" even if the candidate is open to other arrangements. Do not select permanent full time merely to show flexibility.

### Notice period

If the candidate is not employed and can start on the program date, choose "Other" and enter a short factual phrase such as "Available from the program start date" or "No notice period". Do not enter "day shift" because that is availability, not notice.

### Salary expectation

Use advertised pay when available. Match the field format and character limit. If the internship advertises an hourly minimum, enter a concise numeric figure only if the field accepts it, such as `37` or `37/hr`. Do not convert to annual salary unless the form clearly asks for annual base salary. If the form forces an annual number, calculate it transparently and state the assumptions before entering it.

### Education, WAM, completion, and discipline

Use the most recent current qualification and official records. Choose the range containing the exact current WAM. Use the expected completion date, not the date of the internship. For a dual degree, follow the form's instruction about the preferred role stream.

### Residence and preferred location

Choose the nearest listed metropolitan category when the suburb is part of that city. Use "Other" only when none of the named cities reasonably applies.

### Address fields

Put street number and street name in Address Line 1. Put apartment, unit, building, level, or other secondary delivery detail in Address Line 2. Leave Line 2 blank when there is no secondary detail. Never retrieve or repeat a private home address from a reusable Skill.

## Final form review

Compare form answers against the resume, cover letter, visa evidence, academic transcript, and job advertisement. Resolve every contradiction before submission.
